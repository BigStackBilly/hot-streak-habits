Wordday — Support

My streak/stats disappeared

All data is stored locally on your device only, nothing is backed up to a server. This means uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage from your phone's settings, permanently removes your progress. Reinstalling a fresh copy of the app starts empty by design — there's no account to sign back into and recover it from.

Why did my streak reset to 0?

The streak counts consecutive days you've solved the daily puzzle. Missing a day, or failing to guess it within 6 tries, resets the streak — that's by design, not a bug. Solving today's puzzle always starts a fresh streak.

What's the difference between Daily and Endless?

Daily is one puzzle a day, the same word for everyone, and it's what counts toward your streak. Endless is unlimited practice with random words — play as many rounds as you like, it doesn't affect your daily streak or stats.

Can I play more than once a day?

The daily puzzle is once per day by design — that's what makes it a shared daily puzzle rather than an endless game. Switch to Endless mode any time you want to keep playing.

What counts as a valid guess?

Any five-letter word from Wordday's built-in word list. If a guess is rejected, it's either too short or not in that list — try a different common English word.

Can I use this on more than one device?

Not yet — each installation keeps its own separate local data, with no sync between devices. This is a deliberate, current limitation rather than a bug.

Something's broken, or I have a question not covered here

Email hotstreakhabits@gmail.com and describe what happened, including your phone model and iOS version if it's a display or crash issue.
