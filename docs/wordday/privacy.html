Wordday — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

Wordday is a daily word puzzle built to work without collecting your personal data. This page explains exactly what does and doesn't happen with your information.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no account, no login, and no server that Wordday sends your data to. Your guesses, streak, statistics, and theme choice are stored only in your device's local storage. We never see this information, and it never leaves your device.

What we don't do

  • No account creation or login of any kind
  • No advertising or ad tracking
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No selling or sharing of data, because none is collected

Sharing your result

The "Copy result" button builds a short text summary of your guesses entirely on your device and copies it to your clipboard. Nothing is sent to us or any third party as part of this — where you paste it afterward is up to you.

In-app purchases

Tile color themes, when available for purchase, are processed entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play. We do not receive, see, or store your payment details — that's handled directly between you and the platform you downloaded the app from.

Third-party services

The app loads its core libraries from a third-party content delivery network (unpkg). This provider may log standard technical information such as IP address and browser type as part of serving those files, under its own privacy policy. We don't control or receive this information ourselves.

Children's privacy

Wordday is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, regardless of age — since it doesn't collect information at all.

If you delete the app

Uninstalling the app or clearing your browser's site data removes all locally stored progress permanently. We have no copy to restore, since we never had one.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, this page will be updated first, with a new "last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hotstreakhabits@gmail.com.
