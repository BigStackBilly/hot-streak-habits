## Description

```
Hot Streak Habits helps you build the habits that matter — one honest check-in at a time.

Add a habit, pick an icon, choose how often it's due (daily, every few days, or weekly), and tap it off each time you follow through. Watch your streak grow, and feel it when it doesn't.

WHY IT'S DIFFERENT

- No login, no account, no cloud — everything stays on your device
- No ads, no tracking, nothing sold
- A real streak mechanic — miss your window and the streak actually breaks, because that's the whole point
- When a streak breaks, you're asked to sit with why you started before you can just continue — no silent resets
- Flexible plans — daily habits, or anything up to once a week, on a simple slider
- A week-at-a-glance view for every habit, so you can see the pattern, not just today
- Gentle reminders when something's due — never more than once a day, never naggy
- Dark mode, because most habit-building happens at odd hours

BUILT TO STAY OUT OF YOUR WAY

This isn't a productivity system with fifty settings. It's fast enough to use one-handed in under ten seconds, and it stays quiet the rest of the time.

Optional cosmetic flame themes are available if you want to customize the look — entirely optional, never required.

Start your first streak today.
```
