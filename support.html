🔥 Hot Streak Habits — Support

My habits/streaks disappeared

All data is stored locally on your device only, nothing is backed up to a server. This means uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage from your phone's settings, permanently removes your habit data. Reinstalling a fresh copy of the app starts empty by design — there's no account to sign back into and recover it from.

I'm not getting reminders

Reminders only fire while the app is actually open or recently open — there's no background server pushing notifications to a closed app. Make sure notifications are allowed for the app in your phone's Settings, and that the bell icon in the app's header isn't muted. Individual habits can also be muted from their own bell icon without affecting the visual "due today" indicator.

Can I use this on more than one device?

Not yet — each installation keeps its own separate local data, with no sync between devices. This is a deliberate, current limitation rather than a bug.

How do I delete a habit?

Tap the edit (pencil) icon on any habit card, then "Delete habit" at the bottom of that screen.

Something's broken, or I have a question not covered here

Email hotstreakhabits@gmail.com and describe what happened, including your phone model and iOS version if it's a display or crash issue.

See also the privacy policy for details on what data the app does (and doesn't) collect.
