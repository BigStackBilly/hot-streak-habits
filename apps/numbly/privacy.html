Privacy Policy

Numbly · Last updated 7 August 2026

Short version: Numbly has no account, no server and no analytics. Everything you do stays on your device. We never see it.

What we collect

Nothing. Numbly does not collect, transmit or store any personal data on any server. There is no sign-up, no login and no user account.

What stays on your device

Numbly is a number puzzle that refreshes once a day. The app saves your puzzle scores, solve rate, streak and theme choice in your device's local storage. This data:

  • never leaves your device
  • is not readable by us or anyone else
  • is deleted if you delete the app

You can export a copy of it at any time from the app's Backup screen — that export is free and always will be. The file is saved wherever you choose; we never receive it.

Analytics and tracking

None. There is no analytics SDK, no advertising identifier, no crash reporter and no third-party tracking of any kind.

Advertising

Numbly contains no advertising.

Children

Numbly is suitable for all ages. Because it collects nothing, there is no personal information of a child (or anyone else) for us to hold.

Purchases

Optional cosmetic unlocks may be offered inside the app. If you buy one, the payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google — we never see your payment details.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated date at the top of this page will change with it.
