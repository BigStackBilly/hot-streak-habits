Support

Numbly · Last updated 7 August 2026

Numbly is a number puzzle that refreshes once a day. No account, no ads, no tracking — everything stays on your device.

Getting started

Open the app and play. There is nothing to set up, no account to create and no permissions to grant.

Will I lose my progress?

Your progress lives in your device's local storage. It survives closing and reopening the app, but it is removed if you delete the app or clear its data. Before switching phones, use Backup → Export my data and keep the file somewhere safe. On the new device, use Restore from file.

Why is there only one puzzle a day?

That is the point — everybody gets the same one, so scores are comparable. If you want to keep playing, the practice mode is unlimited and completely free. Practice rounds never affect your daily score, stats or streak.

My streak disappeared

A streak counts consecutive calendar days on your device. Missing a day resets it. Changing your device's date or time zone can also affect which day the app thinks it is.

Do I have to pay for anything?

No. The whole game is free. Optional unlocks are cosmetic colour themes only — they never change the puzzles, the difficulty or your score.

Something is broken

Email hotstreakhabits@gmail.com with your device model and iOS or Android version, and a short description of what happened. Screenshots help a lot.
