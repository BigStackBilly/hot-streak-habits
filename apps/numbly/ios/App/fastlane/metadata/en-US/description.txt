Numbly deals you six numbers and one target. Combine any two with plus, minus, times or divide to make a new number, and keep going until you hit the target exactly.

WHY IT'S DIFFERENT

- No login, no account, no cloud — everything stays on your device
- No ads, no tracking, nothing sold
- Every daily puzzle is generated from a real solution, so it is always solvable
- Partial credit for getting close, and a speed bonus for solving it fast
- Undo any step, as many times as you like
- Unlimited free practice puzzles whenever you want
- Dark mode and light mode, your choice

A handful of optional colour themes are available if you want them — entirely optional, never required, and they never change the numbers or your score. A free backup & restore is always available too — this is your data, not a premium feature.
