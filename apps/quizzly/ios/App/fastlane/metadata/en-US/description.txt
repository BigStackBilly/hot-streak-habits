Quizzly is a ten-question trivia gauntlet. Everyone gets the same ten questions on the same day, so a score is worth comparing.

WHY IT'S DIFFERENT

- No login, no account, no cloud — everything stays on your device
- No ads, no tracking, nothing sold
- The same ten questions for everyone each day
- Answers are revealed as you go, so you actually learn something
- Unlimited free practice rounds whenever you want
- Dark mode and light mode, your choice

A handful of optional colour themes are available if you want them — entirely optional, never required, and they never change the questions or your score. A free backup & restore is always available too — this is your data, not a premium feature.
