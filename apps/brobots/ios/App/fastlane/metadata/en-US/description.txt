Brobots is a drop-and-merge puzzle: aim, release, and watch two matching robots fuse into one bigger one. Keep merging, keep the board clear, and don't let the stack cross the line at the top.

HOW TO PLAY

Drag anywhere to aim, release to drop the robot shown in "Next." Two robots of the same color and size merge the instant they touch — Nanobot into Microbot, all the way up to the mighty Omegabot. Chain reactions cascade for big scores. Misjudge it too many times and the stack fills the screen — game over.
