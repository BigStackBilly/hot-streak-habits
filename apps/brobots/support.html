🤖 Brobots — Support

How do I play?

Drag anywhere in the arena to aim, then release to drop the robot shown in the "Next" preview. Two robots of the same color and size fuse into the next bigger one when they touch. The run ends if the stack settles above the dashed line near the top.

My high score disappeared

Your high score is stored locally on your device only, nothing is backed up to a server. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage from your phone's settings, permanently removes it. Reinstalling starts fresh by design — there's no account to sign back into and recover it from. Use Backup & Restore (the disk icon) beforehand if you want to keep it.

Why can't I drop another robot right away?

There's a short cooldown after each drop so the board has a moment to settle before the next one — this is deliberate pacing, not lag.

What does the free plan include?

Everything needed to play is free, forever — no cap, no ads, no timers. "Brobots Pro" is a one-time unlock for 3 extra cosmetic robot color packs (Neon, Sunset, Galaxy). Purely visual, never required to play or compete for a high score.

Can I use this on more than one device?

Not yet — each installation keeps its own separate local high score, with no sync between devices. This is a deliberate, current limitation rather than a bug.
