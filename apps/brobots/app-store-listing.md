## Description

```
Brobots is a drop-and-merge puzzle: aim, release, and watch two matching robots fuse into one bigger one. Keep merging, keep the board clear, and don't let the stack cross the line at the top.

HOW TO PLAY

Drag anywhere to aim, release to drop the robot shown in "Next." Two robots of the same color and size merge the instant they touch — Nanobot into Microbot, all the way up to the mighty Omegabot. Chain reactions cascade for big scores. Misjudge it too many times and the stack fills the screen — game over.

WHY IT'S DIFFERENT

- No login, no account, no cloud — everything stays on your device
- No ads, no tracking, nothing sold
- Real physics, not a fixed grid — every drop lands and settles differently
- Honest high-score tracking, saved locally, with a "new high score" callout only when it's actually true
- Optional cosmetic robot color packs (Neon, Sunset, Galaxy) if you want to customize the look — entirely optional, never required
- Free local backup and restore, so your high score is never trapped on one device
- Dark mode

BUILT TO STAY OUT OF YOUR WAY

No lives to wait out, no energy timer, no forced ads between rounds. Just tap in, play a round, tap out.

Drop your first robot today.
```
