## Description

```
Tally is a fast, no-nonsense bill-splitting and tip calculator.

Type in a bill subtotal on a big thumb-friendly keypad. Pick a tip — quick chips for 15/18/20/25%, a custom entry, or a slider, whichever's fastest for you. Set how many people are splitting it with a simple +/- stepper. The tip amount, total, and per-person share update instantly as you go — no "calculate" button, ever.

WHY IT'S DIFFERENT

- The calculator itself is completely free, always — no ads, no paywall on the core feature
- No login, no account, no cloud — everything stays on your device
- Live math, every keystroke — tip, total, and per-person amounts recompute instantly
- "Round up per person" — rounds each share up to a whole currency unit, handy for cash tipping, and shows exactly how much extra that collects
- Remembers your last-used tip percentage and person count as a starting point next time
- An optional, lightweight history — save a split with a venue name and revisit it later, entirely secondary to the fast calculator
- Pick your currency symbol; switch between a crisp dark or light look

BUILT TO STAY OUT OF YOUR WAY

No accounts, no forced tutorials, no ads interrupting a bill split at a restaurant table. Open it, type the number, done.

A couple of optional cosmetic keypad colors and unlimited saved-split history are available via Tally Pro — entirely optional, never required to use the calculator.

Split your next bill in seconds.
```
