Fuseday is a tile-merge puzzle with a twist: the Daily Challenge gives everyone the exact same board, once a day, so you can compare notes with friends on the same puzzle.

WHY IT'S DIFFERENT

- No login, no account, no cloud — everything stays on your device
- No ads, no tracking, nothing sold
- Endless mode for unlimited free play whenever you want
- Best score and best tile tracked locally, just for you
- Dark mode and light mode, your choice

A handful of optional cosmetic tile themes are available if you want to change how the board looks — entirely optional, never required. A free backup & restore is always available too — this is your data, not a premium feature.
