🧩 Fuseday — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026

Fuseday is a tile-merge puzzle game built to work without collecting your personal data. This page explains exactly what does and doesn't happen with your information.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no account, no login, and no server that Fuseday sends your data to. Your best score, best tile reached, current game boards, and your daily challenge history are stored only in your device's local storage. We never see this information, and it never leaves your device.

What we don't do

  • No account creation or login of any kind
  • No advertising or ad tracking
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No selling or sharing of data, because none is collected

Daily Challenge

The Daily Challenge board is generated from that calendar day's date using a deterministic algorithm that runs entirely on your device — no data is sent anywhere to fetch or verify today's puzzle.

Sharing your result

The "Share result" button uses your device's own native share sheet (or copies text to your clipboard) to let you send a short text summary of your score to whichever app or contact you choose. Fuseday itself does not transmit this text anywhere.

In-app purchases

Cosmetic tile-color packs, when available for purchase, are processed entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play. We do not receive, see, or store your payment details — that's handled directly between you and the platform you downloaded the app from.

Third-party services

The app loads its fonts and core libraries from third-party content delivery networks (Google Fonts, unpkg). These providers may log standard technical information such as IP address and browser type as part of serving those files, under their own privacy policies. We don't control or receive this information ourselves.

Children's privacy

Fuseday is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, regardless of age — since it doesn't collect information at all.

If you delete the app

Uninstalling the app or clearing your browser's site data removes all locally stored game data permanently. We have no copy to restore, since we never had one.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future feature like cloud sync introduces an account or server-side storage — this page will be updated first, with a new "last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hotstreakhabits@gmail.com.
