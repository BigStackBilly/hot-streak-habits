## Description

```
Fuseday is a tile-merge number puzzle: swipe to slide every tile on the board, matching numbers fuse together into double their value, and the board fills up fast. Simple to learn, hard to put down.

Play ENDLESS mode any time — push your best score and biggest tile as high as you can, with your all-time records tracked right on the home screen.

Or take on the DAILY CHALLENGE — a twist you won't find in most tile-merge games. Every player gets the exact same sequence of tile spawns on a given calendar day, so everyone is racing the same board. Only your swipe choices differ. You get one run a day: play until you get stuck, or lock in your result whenever you want. Then share a short, spoiler-free summary of how you did — just like a word-of-the-day game, but for numbers.

WHY IT'S DIFFERENT

- A genuine daily challenge — a seeded, reproducible puzzle shared by everyone that day, not just endless random play
- Smooth, satisfying tile-slide and merge animations
- Honest personal-best tracking — a "new personal best" callout only appears when it's actually true
- A lightweight history of your past daily results
- Optional cosmetic tile-color packs (Neon, Pastel, Wood, Midnight) if you want to customize the look — entirely optional, never required
- No login, no account, no cloud — everything stays on your device
- No ads, no tracking, nothing sold

BUILT TO STAY OUT OF YOUR WAY

Free local backup and restore means your scores and daily history are never trapped on one device. Dark mode by default, with a light mode if you'd rather play in daylight.

Swipe your first tile today.
```
