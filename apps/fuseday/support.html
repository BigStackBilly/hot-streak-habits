🧩 Fuseday — Support

My best score/history disappeared

All data is stored locally on your device only, nothing is backed up to a server. This means uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage from your phone's settings, permanently removes your progress. Reinstalling a fresh copy of the app starts empty by design — there's no account to sign back into and recover it from. Use the 💾 Backup & restore feature to save a copy before uninstalling or switching devices.

How does the Daily Challenge work?

Every player gets the exact same sequence of tile spawns on a given calendar day, so everyone is effectively playing the same puzzle — only your own swipe choices differ. You get one run per day: either play until you get stuck (game over), or tap "End run" to lock in whatever score you've reached so far. Once you've finished, you can't retry today's puzzle — come back after midnight for a new one.

Can I play the Daily Challenge more than once a day?

No — like a lot of daily puzzle games, it's intentionally once a day. Endless mode is unlimited any time you like.

The Share result button didn't do anything

On a phone, it should open your device's native share sheet. On a desktop browser without that feature, it copies the result text to your clipboard instead and shows a brief "Copied!" confirmation — check your clipboard and paste it wherever you'd like.

Can I use this on more than one device?

Not yet — each installation keeps its own separate local data, with no sync between devices. You can manually move your data between devices with the 💾 Backup & restore feature.
