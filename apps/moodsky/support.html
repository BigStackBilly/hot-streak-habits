✨ Mood Star — Support

My stars/streak disappeared

All data is stored locally on your device only, nothing is backed up to a server. This means uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage from your phone's settings, permanently removes your mood history. Reinstalling a fresh copy of the app starts empty by design — there's no account to sign back into and recover it from.

I'm not getting reminders

Reminders only fire while the app is actually open or recently open — there's no background server pushing notifications to a closed app. Make sure notifications are allowed for the app in your phone's Settings, and that the bell icon in the app's header isn't muted.

Can I log a day I forgot, or fix a mistake?

You can only log a mood for today — Mood Star is built around today's honest snapshot, not backfilling history. If you tap the wrong mood today, just tap the right one; it overwrites. Tapping any star in your sky opens that day's mood and note, and you can delete it from there if you logged it by mistake.

Can I use this on more than one device?

Not yet — each installation keeps its own separate local data, with no sync between devices. This is a deliberate, current limitation rather than a bug.

Something's broken, or I have a question not covered here

Email hotstreakhabits@gmail.com and describe what happened, including your phone model and iOS version if it's a display or crash issue.

See also the privacy policy for details on what data the app does (and doesn't) collect.
