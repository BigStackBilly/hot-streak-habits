Mood Sky turns checking in on yourself into something you actually want to see grow.

Once a day, tap the mood that fits — that's it, one tap. Add a single word if you want to remember why, or skip it. That day becomes a star, placed into your own night sky, glowing in a color that matches how you felt.

Come back day after day and watch your sky fill in — a personal constellation you can scroll back through, month by month, tapping any past star to see what that day looked like.

WHY IT'S DIFFERENT

- No login, no account, no cloud — everything stays on your device
- No ads, no tracking, nothing sold
- Genuinely one-tap — pick a mood, your star is placed, done
- A real streak mechanic, tracked honestly — miss a day and it resets, no fudging
- A simple stats view — your mood mix over the last 30 days, current streak, longest streak
- A dark night-sky theme by default, with a soft "dawn" look for daytime
- Gentle reminders if you haven't logged today — never more than once a day, never naggy

BUILT TO STAY OUT OF YOUR WAY

This isn't a mood journal with fifty fields to fill in. It's fast enough to use one-handed in under five seconds, and the payoff — your own growing sky — is something you'll want to check back in on.

Optional cosmetic sky themes (Aurora, Sunset, Deep Space) are available if you want to customize the look — entirely optional, never required. A free backup & restore is always available too — this is your data, not a premium feature.
