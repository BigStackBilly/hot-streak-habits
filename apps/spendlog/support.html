🪙 Spendlog — Support

My expenses disappeared

All data is stored locally on your device only, nothing is backed up to a server. This means uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage from your phone's settings, permanently removes your expense history. Reinstalling a fresh copy of the app starts empty by design — there's no account to sign back into and recover it from. Use the free "Backup & restore" option in the app (the 💾 icon) regularly to save a copy you can bring back later.

Can I connect my bank account?

No — Spendlog is intentionally manual-entry only, with no bank or card linking of any kind. Every expense is typed in by hand, on purpose, so nothing about your finances is ever shared with a third party.

Can I fix or delete a past entry?

Yes. Tap any entry in the "Recent" list to reopen it — from there you can change the amount, category, or note, or delete it entirely. Totals and the category breakdown update immediately.

What does Spendlog Pro unlock?

Pro adds unlimited custom categories (your own icon, label, and color) and the ability to export every logged expense to a real CSV file. The seven starter categories and all core logging/editing features are free and always available, with no ads and no tracking either way.

How do I change the currency symbol?

Open Settings (the ⚙️ icon) and pick from the available currency symbols. This only changes how amounts are displayed — Spendlog doesn't do currency conversion.

Can I use this on more than one device?

Not automatically — each installation keeps its own separate local data, with no sync between devices. Use the free backup/restore feature to manually move your data from one device to another.

Something's broken, or I have a question not covered here

Email hotstreakhabits@gmail.com and describe what happened, including your phone model and OS version if it's a display or crash issue.
