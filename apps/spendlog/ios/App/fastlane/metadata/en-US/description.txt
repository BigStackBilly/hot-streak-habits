TapSpend is a personal expense logger built around one idea: if logging a purchase takes longer than ten seconds, you'll stop doing it.

WHY IT'S DIFFERENT

- No bank linking — nothing to connect, nothing to authorise, no read access to your accounts
- No login, no account, no cloud — everything stays on your device
- No ads, no tracking, nothing sold
- Amount, category, done. That's the whole flow
- See where the money actually went, by category and by month
- Dark mode and light mode, your choice

Extra categories and a longer history view are available as an optional unlock if you want them — entirely optional, never required. A free backup & restore is always available too — this is your data, not a premium feature.
