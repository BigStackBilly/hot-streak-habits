## Description

```
Spendlog is a dead-simple expense logger built around one idea: logging a purchase should never take more than 10 seconds.

Tap the + button, punch in the amount on a big thumb-friendly keypad, tap a category, optionally add a short note, and you're done. No bank account to link, no login screen, no cloud sync — every entry lives only on your device.

WHAT YOU GET

- A big custom numeric keypad built for speed, not the fiddly OS keyboard
- Seven starter categories: Food, Transport, Shopping, Home, Fun, Health, Other
- Running totals for Today, This Week, and This Month, always visible
- A simple category breakdown showing where this month's spending is going
- Tap any past entry to edit or delete it — totals update instantly
- Free backup & restore, any time — export your whole log to a file and bring it back later
- No daily streak, no guilt trips — spending isn't a daily ritual, and Spendlog doesn't pretend it is

HONEST ABOUT WHAT IT ISN'T

Spendlog does not connect to your bank, does not categorize transactions automatically, and does not do budgeting forecasts. It's a fast manual log, nothing more — if that's what you actually want, this is built for you.

SPENDLOG PRO (optional)

- Unlimited custom categories — your own icon, label, and color
- Export every entry to a real CSV file, any time

The core app — logging, editing, totals, breakdown, and backup — is free and complete without Pro.

Log your first expense in less time than it took to read this.
```
