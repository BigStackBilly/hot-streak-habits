🪙 TapSpend — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

TapSpend is a personal expense logger built to work without collecting your personal data. This page explains exactly what does and doesn't happen with your information.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no account, no login, and no server that TapSpend sends your data to. Every expense you log — the amount, category, optional note, and time — is stored only in your device's local storage. We never see this information, and it never leaves your device.

No bank linking

TapSpend never connects to any bank account, card, or financial institution. Every entry is typed in by hand; there is no automatic transaction import of any kind.

What we don't do

  • No account creation or login of any kind
  • No advertising or ad tracking
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No selling or sharing of data, because none is collected

In-app purchases

The optional Pro unlock (custom categories and CSV export) is processed entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play when available for purchase. We do not receive, see, or store your payment details — that's handled directly between you and the platform you downloaded the app from.

Third-party services

The app loads its fonts and core libraries from third-party content delivery networks (Google Fonts, unpkg). These providers may log standard technical information such as IP address and browser type as part of serving those files, under their own privacy policies. We don't control or receive this information ourselves.

Children's privacy

TapSpend is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, regardless of age — since it doesn't collect information at all.

If you delete the app

Uninstalling the app or clearing your browser's site data removes all locally stored expense data permanently. We have no copy to restore, since we never had one — use the free built-in backup/restore feature to keep your own copy.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future feature like cloud sync introduces an account or server-side storage — this page will be updated first, with a new "last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hotstreakhabits@gmail.com.
