How to play

Creeps walk the road from the gate to your Command Center. Every one that arrives takes a bite out of it. Hold for twenty waves and you win.

  • Build a reactor first. Nothing can be built outside a power field, and each reactor costs more than the last — so pick your ground rather than spreading thin.
  • Put extractors on the crystal seams early. They pay for themselves in about fifty seconds.
  • Mix your guns. Siege cannons splash hard but cannot hit air. Gun turrets hit anything but bounce off armour. Cryo emitters barely scratch anything and make everything else work. Tesla coils want a crowd.
  • Use your marines. A creep that walks into one stops walking and fights it. Right-click to move the rally flag into a choke under your guns.
  • Call waves in early when you are ahead — the seconds you don't use are paid out in minerals.

Common questions

Can I save a run and come back to it?
No. A run takes about ten minutes and is meant to be played in one sitting. Your best wave per map, your Daily Challenge scores and your streak are all saved permanently.
What is the Daily Challenge?
One run a day, the same map and the same two modifiers for everybody, worked out from the date itself. Only your first attempt scores — you can replay it, but the replay won't overwrite the result. Playing on consecutive days builds a streak.
I lost my streak / my best waves.
Those are stored only on your own device. Clearing your browser's site data, using a private window, or reinstalling the app will erase them, and there is no server copy to restore from. Sorry — that's the trade for a game with no accounts.
The map looks slightly blurry on my phone.
The game is drawn 384 pixels wide. On a narrower screen it scales down fractionally so you can see the whole map, which softens the pixels a little. Turning the phone to landscape, or playing on a larger screen, gives you the sharp version.
There's no sound.
Check the speaker button in the top right. Browsers also require you to tap or click once before any audio can start.
Are there ads or purchases?
No. There are no ads, no purchases, no accounts and no tracking. See the privacy policy.
