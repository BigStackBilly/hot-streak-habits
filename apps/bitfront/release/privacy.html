Bitfront — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 August 2026

The short version: Bitfront collects nothing, sends nothing, and has no servers. Everything the game remembers is stored on your own device and never leaves it.

What we collect

Nothing. Bitfront has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs. We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, device identifiers or gameplay data, because there is no server for any of it to be sent to.

What is stored on your device

The game saves a small amount of data locally, using your browser or device's own storage. It stays on the device and is only readable by the game itself:

  • your best wave reached, for each map
  • your Daily Challenge scores and your current streak
  • which map you last chose
  • whether you muted the sound

Deleting the app, or clearing your browser's site data, erases all of it permanently. We have no copy, so there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf and nothing we could restore.

The Daily Challenge

The Daily Challenge gives everyone the same run on the same day. It does this by generating the run from the calendar date itself, on your device. Nothing is downloaded and no score is uploaded. There is no leaderboard and no comparison with other players except the one you make by pasting your result somewhere yourself.

Sharing your result

The "share result" button copies a few lines of text to your clipboard. Where you paste it after that is entirely your choice; the game does not send it anywhere.

Children

Bitfront is suitable for general audiences and contains no chat, no user accounts, no purchases, no advertising and no gambling or loot-box mechanics. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we collect none from children either.

Network access

The browser version loads one optional web font from Google Fonts for its on-screen text; if that request fails, the game falls back to a built-in font and plays normally. Aside from loading the game files themselves, Bitfront makes no other network requests, and it runs fully offline once loaded.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new "last updated" date. If a future version of the game ever does collect data, that will be stated here plainly and in advance.
