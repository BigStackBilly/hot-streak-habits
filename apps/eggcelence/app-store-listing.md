## Description

```
Eggcelence is the fastest way to time an egg. No setup screen, no confirmation dialog — tap the style you want and the timer starts immediately.

TAP AND GO
9 built-in presets cover the classics: Soft, Jammy, Medium, and Hard boiled; Poached; Sunny side up and Over easy fried; Scrambled; and Baked (shirred). Each button shows a custom icon, the style name, and the exact time at a glance, so you can tap without thinking.

A BIG, HONEST COUNTDOWN
Once a timer starts, the screen goes full-screen with a large MM:SS countdown and a filling ring so you always know how much time is left. When it hits zero, you get an unmistakable "Done!" screen, a chime, and a vibration.

WORKS EVEN WHEN YOUR PHONE ISN'T
A kitchen timer that only works while you stare at the screen isn't very useful. Eggcelence schedules a real device notification the moment you start a timer, so you'll get alerted even if your phone is locked or you've switched to another app.

MORE THAN THE CLASSICS
Need something the presets don't cover? A secondary custom timer (up to 15 minutes) handles anything else. An optional Extra Eggs pack adds four more styles — Ramen, Quail, Sous vide, and Century egg.

NO PRESSURE, NO TRACKING
No login, no account, no cloud, no ads. A light "eggs timed" counter is the only nod to progress — there's no streak or daily-pressure mechanic, because a kitchen timer isn't a habit. Free backup & restore keeps your data safe across devices, always.

Tap an egg. Walk away. Get alerted.
```
