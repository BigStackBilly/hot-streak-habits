🥚 Eggcelence — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026

Eggcelence is an egg-timer app built to work without collecting your personal data. This page explains exactly what does and doesn't happen with your information.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no account, no login, and no server that Eggcelence sends your data to. Your lifetime eggs-timed count, theme choice, and Extra Eggs pack unlock are stored only in your device's local storage. We never see this information, and it never leaves your device.

What we don't do

  • No account creation or login of any kind
  • No advertising or ad tracking
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No selling or sharing of data, because none is collected

Notifications

When you start a timer, the app schedules a local notification directly on your own device (via your OS's local-notification system when installed as a native app, or a best-effort browser notification otherwise) so it can alert you when the timer finishes — including if the app is backgrounded. This notification is generated and delivered entirely on-device. No timer data, notification content, or usage information is sent to or through any server we operate.

In-app purchases

The optional "Extra Eggs" preset pack, when available for purchase, is processed entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play. We do not receive, see, or store your payment details — that's handled directly between you and the platform you downloaded the app from.

Third-party services

The app loads its fonts and core libraries from third-party content delivery networks (Google Fonts, unpkg). These providers may log standard technical information such as IP address and browser type as part of serving those files, under their own privacy policies. We don't control or receive this information ourselves.

Children's privacy

Eggcelence is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, regardless of age — since it doesn't collect information at all.

If you delete the app

Uninstalling the app or clearing your browser's site data removes all locally stored data permanently, including your eggs-timed count. We have no copy to restore, since we never had one.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future feature like cloud sync introduces an account or server-side storage — this page will be updated first, with a new "last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hotstreakhabits@gmail.com.
