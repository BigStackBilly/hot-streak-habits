Reflexly is a four-stage reflex gauntlet. Each stage tests something different, and everyone gets the same run on the same day.

WHY IT'S DIFFERENT

- No login, no account, no cloud — everything stays on your device
- No ads, no tracking, nothing sold
- The same four stages for everyone each day, so times are comparable
- Four different kinds of reaction, not the same tap repeated
- Unlimited free practice runs whenever you want
- Dark mode and light mode, your choice

A handful of optional colour themes are available if you want them — entirely optional, never required, and they never change the stages or your score. A free backup & restore is always available too — this is your data, not a premium feature.
