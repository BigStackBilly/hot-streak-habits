〰️ Stillwaterz — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

Stillwaterz is a guided breathing timer built to work without collecting your personal data. This page explains exactly what does and doesn't happen with your information.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no account, no login, and no server that Stillwaterz sends your data to. Your session history — which breathing pattern you used, how long you practiced, and how many breath cycles you completed — is stored only in your device's local storage. We never see this information, and it never leaves your device.

What we don't do

  • No account creation or login of any kind
  • No advertising or ad tracking
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No microphone or camera access of any kind
  • No selling or sharing of data, because none is collected

Audio

The ambient sounds and phase chimes are generated entirely on your device using the Web Audio API. Nothing is recorded, and no audio ever leaves your device.

In-app purchases

Extra breathing patterns and ambient sound packs, when available for purchase, are processed entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play. We do not receive, see, or store your payment details — that's handled directly between you and the platform you downloaded the app from.

Third-party services

The app loads its fonts and core libraries from third-party content delivery networks (Google Fonts, unpkg). These providers may log standard technical information such as IP address and browser type as part of serving those files, under their own privacy policies. We don't control or receive this information ourselves.

Children's privacy

Stillwaterz is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, regardless of age — since it doesn't collect information at all.

If you delete the app

Uninstalling the app or clearing your browser's site data removes all locally stored session history permanently. We have no copy to restore, since we never had one. You can make your own backup at any time using the free Backup & Restore feature in the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future feature like cloud sync introduces an account or server-side storage — this page will be updated first, with a new "last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hotstreakhabits@gmail.com.

This page is intentionally short because the app itself is intentionally simple: everything stays on your device, always.
