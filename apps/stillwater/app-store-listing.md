## Description

```
Stillwater is a quiet place to slow your breathing down.

Pick a well-known breathing pattern — box breathing (used to stay calm under pressure), 4-7-8 (a favorite for easing into sleep), or a simpler even in-and-out rhythm — and follow a slowly expanding and contracting circle synced exactly to its timing. A soft label tells you what to do at each step: breathe in, hold, breathe out, hold.

Turn on the generated ambient sound — a soft, low pad, entirely synthesized on your device — and gentle chimes mark each phase change, so you can close your eyes and just listen.

WHY IT'S DIFFERENT

- No login, no account, no cloud — everything stays on your device
- No ads, no tracking, nothing sold
- No streaks, no pressure, no gamification — this app is built to calm you down, not to make you feel behind
- Choose a session length (1, 3, 5, or 10 minutes, or run until you stop) and just breathe
- A calming ambient tone generated live with no audio files, plus optional soft phase chimes
- A dark, low-light theme by default, with a soft muted daytime look available too
- A very light "sessions this week" note if you want a small sense of progress — never a loud number or badge

BUILT TO STAY OUT OF YOUR WAY

Every core breathing pattern and the full guided session experience is free and unlimited — the whole point of this app would be undercut by putting the calming part behind a paywall. A couple of extra patterns and ambient sound colors (Ocean-like, Deep hum, Soft rain) are available if you want more variety — entirely optional, never required.

Take a minute. Breathe.
```
