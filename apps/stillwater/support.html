My session history disappeared

All data is stored locally on your device only, nothing is backed up to a server. This means uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage from your phone's settings, permanently removes your session history. Reinstalling a fresh copy of the app starts empty by design — there's no account to sign back into and recover it from. If you made a backup first (see Backup & Restore in the app), you can restore from that file instead.

I don't hear any sound

Check the speaker icon in the app's top bar isn't muted, and that your phone's silent switch/media volume is turned up — the ambient sound and phase chimes play through your device's normal media volume, not the ringer. Sound also only starts once you tap "Begin session," since phones require a real tap before a page is allowed to play audio.

Can I keep a session going in the background?

The guided session runs while the app is open and the screen is on. Like most browser-based timers, it may pause if you lock your phone or fully switch away from the app mid-session.

Can I use this on more than one device?

Not yet — each installation keeps its own separate local data, with no sync between devices. This is a deliberate, current limitation rather than a bug. Use Backup & Restore to manually move your history between devices.
