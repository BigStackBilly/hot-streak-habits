🏋️ Gains Log — Support

My workouts/personal records disappeared

All data is stored locally on your device only, nothing is backed up to a server. This means uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage from your phone's settings, permanently removes your data. Reinstalling a fresh copy of the app starts empty by design — there's no account to sign back into and recover it from. Use Backup & Restore (the save icon in the header) regularly if you want a safety copy.

How does the personal-record callout work?

A set is flagged as a PR when its weight beats the heaviest weight you've ever logged at that exact rep count, across your saved workout history. A lighter or equal-weight set at the same rep count never triggers it, and the very first time you log a rep count doesn't count as a PR either — there's nothing on record yet to beat.

Why doesn't it push me to work out every day?

That's deliberate. Most lifters follow a split (push/pull/legs, 3x/week, etc.), not a daily habit — so Gains Log just shows a simple count of workouts this week and this month, with no streak to lose and no guilt mechanic.

What does "Same as last set" do?

It logs another set with the exact same weight and reps as the one you just logged for that exercise, in one tap — handy for back-to-back identical sets without touching the steppers again.

Can I use this on more than one device?

Not yet — each installation keeps its own separate local data, with no sync between devices. This is a deliberate, current limitation rather than a bug. Backup & Restore can move your data between devices manually.

What does the free plan include?

The full default exercise library, unlimited workouts, and unlimited history are always free — nothing about actually logging a workout is gated. The free plan caps custom exercises at 3; "Gains Log Pro" removes that cap and adds two extra accent colors. It's a one-time local unlock in the current version of the app; see the Pro screen (crown icon) for details.

How do I delete a workout?

Tap a workout in your history to open it, then "Delete workout" at the bottom.
