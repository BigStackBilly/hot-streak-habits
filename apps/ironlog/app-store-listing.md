## Description

```
Ironlog is a strength-training workout log for people who actually lift — sets, reps, and weight, tracked honestly, without turning your training into another app to manage.

Start a workout, pick your exercises from a solid built-in library (or add your own), and log each set in seconds with a fast weight/reps stepper — including a one-tap "same as last set" for back-to-back working sets. Finish up and it's saved to your history for good.

WHY IT'S DIFFERENT

- A genuine personal-record callout — beat your best weight at a given rep count and Ironlog tells you right then, with a real fanfare, not a vague "nice job"
- No login, no account, no cloud — everything stays on your device
- No ads, no tracking, nothing sold
- No punishing daily streak — a simple "workouts this week/month" count instead, because lifters follow splits, not daily check-ins
- Full per-exercise history — every past session's sets, plus a personal-record table by rep count
- Free local backup and restore — your training log is never trapped on one device
- Dark, high-contrast, gym-appropriate design by default
- Fast enough to log a whole workout one-handed between sets

BUILT TO STAY OUT OF YOUR WAY

This isn't a social fitness network with feeds and challenges. It's a log — open it, log your sets, close it, go lift.

The default exercise library, unlimited workouts, and full history are free forever. Ironlog Pro adds unlimited custom exercises (free plan: 3) plus two extra accent colors, as a one-time unlock, not a subscription.

Start your first workout today.
```
