## Description

```
Gembloom is a classic match-3 puzzle: swap two neighboring gems to line up 3 or more, watch them clear, and chase the cascades that follow.

HOW TO PLAY

Tap a gem, then tap a neighbor to swap. Line up 3+ gems of the same color and they clear, scoring points and dropping fresh gems in from above — if that causes another match, it chains into a bigger combo. Swaps that don't make a match bounce back for free, so there's no penalty for experimenting. Each round gives you a set number of moves; score as high as you can before they run out.

WHY IT'S DIFFERENT

- No login, no account, no cloud — everything stays on your device
- No ads, no tracking, nothing sold
- No lives to wait out, no energy timer, no forced ads between rounds
- Honest high-score tracking, saved locally, with a "new high score" callout only when it's actually true
- Optional cosmetic gem color packs (Pastel, Neon, Midnight) if you want to customize the look — entirely optional, never required
- Free local backup and restore, so your high score is never trapped on one device
- Dark mode

BUILT TO STAY OUT OF YOUR WAY

Tap in, play a round, tap out — no forced tutorials, no popups begging for a review.

Swap your first gem today.
```
