💎 Gembloom — Support

How do I play?

Tap a gem, then tap a neighboring gem to swap them. If the swap lines up 3 or more of the same gem in a row or column, they clear and score points, and new gems fall in from the top — chains from that cascade score extra. If a swap doesn't make a line, it reverts and doesn't cost you a move. The round ends when you run out of moves.

My high score disappeared

Your high score is stored locally on your device only, nothing is backed up to a server. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage from your phone's settings, permanently removes it. Reinstalling starts fresh by design — there's no account to sign back into and recover it from. Use Backup & Restore (the disk icon) beforehand if you want to keep it.

Why didn't my swap use up a move?

Only a swap that actually creates a match counts. Try a swap that doesn't line anything up and it just bounces back — free to experiment.

What does the free plan include?

Everything needed to play is free, forever — no cap, no ads, no timers. "Gembloom Pro" is a one-time unlock for 3 extra cosmetic gem color packs (Pastel, Neon, Midnight). Purely visual, never required to play or compete for a high score.

Can I use this on more than one device?

Not yet — each installation keeps its own separate local high score, with no sync between devices. This is a deliberate, current limitation rather than a bug.
