Gembloom is a classic match-3 puzzle: swap two neighboring gems to line up 3 or more, watch them clear, and chase the cascades that follow.

HOW TO PLAY

Tap a gem, then tap a neighbor to swap. Line up 3+ gems of the same color and they clear, scoring points and dropping fresh gems in from above — if that causes another match, it chains into a bigger combo. Swaps that don't make a match bounce back for free, so there's no penalty for experimenting. Each round gives you a set number of moves; score as high as you can before they run out.
