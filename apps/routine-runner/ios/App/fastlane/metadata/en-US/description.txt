Routine Runner turns your morning, bedtime, or any everyday checklist into a guided, step-by-step run-through — so you never have to hold the whole list in your head.

Build a routine (like "Morning" or "Bedtime"), add each small step in order — brush teeth, stretch, drink water — and optionally give any step a short built-in timer. Then just hit Start: one step fills the screen at a time, with a big tap-to-advance area, a live countdown where you've set one, and a progress bar so you always know how far you've got left.

WHY IT'S DIFFERENT

- No login, no account, no cloud — everything stays on your device
- No ads, no tracking, nothing sold
- A real streak mechanic — finish every step to bank the day; a partial run honestly doesn't count
- Full-screen guided mode keeps you moving without re-reading a list
- Per-step timers for anything that needs a beat — stretches, brushing, breathing
- A week-at-a-glance view for every routine, so you can see the pattern, not just today
- Gentle reminders when a routine's ready to run — never more than once a day, never naggy
- Dark mode, because mornings and bedtimes both happen at odd hours

BUILT TO STAY OUT OF YOUR WAY

Free to start with up to 2 routines — plenty for a morning and a bedtime routine. Routine Runner Pro removes that cap for anyone building out more (workout prep, a kid's routine, a work-from-home shutdown ritual, whatever you need). A free backup & restore is always available too — this is your data, not a premium feature.
