🏁 Routine Runner — Support

My routines/streaks disappeared

All data is stored locally on your device only, nothing is backed up to a server. This means uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage from your phone's settings, permanently removes your routine data. Reinstalling a fresh copy of the app starts empty by design — there's no account to sign back into and recover it from.

I'm not getting reminders

Reminders only fire while the app is actually open or recently open — there's no background server pushing notifications to a closed app. Make sure notifications are allowed for the app in your phone's Settings, and that the bell icon in the app's header isn't muted. Individual routines can also be muted from their own bell icon without affecting the visual "due today" indicator.

I canceled a run partway through — why didn't my streak update?

Only a routine you finish start-to-finish counts toward that day's streak. Ending a run early is a deliberate exit — nothing is saved for that day, and you can start the routine again from the beginning any time before midnight.

Can I use this on more than one device?

Not yet — each installation keeps its own separate local data, with no sync between devices. This is a deliberate, current limitation rather than a bug.

How do I delete a routine?

Tap a routine's card to open it, then "Delete routine" at the bottom of that screen.

What does the free plan include?

The free plan supports up to 2 routines with everything else — guided runs, streaks, reminders, dark mode — fully included. "Routine Runner Pro" removes that 2-routine cap. It's a one-time local unlock in the current version of the app; see the Pro screen (crown icon) for details.

Something's broken, or I have a question not covered here

Email hotstreakhabits@gmail.com and describe what happened, including your phone model and iOS version if it's a display or crash issue.

See also the privacy policy for details on what data the app does (and doesn't) collect.
