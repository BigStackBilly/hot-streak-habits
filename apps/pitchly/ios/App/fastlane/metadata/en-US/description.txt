Pitchly plays you a short melody. Tap it back. Each round adds another note, and everyone gets the same melody on the same day.

WHY IT'S DIFFERENT

- No login, no account, no cloud — everything stays on your device
- No ads, no tracking, nothing sold
- The pads are tuned to a pentatonic scale, so no sequence you tap can sound wrong
- Replay the melody as often as you like during your turn — it never costs you anything
- Playable on mute by watching which pads light up
- Unlimited free practice melodies whenever you want
- Dark mode and light mode, your choice

A handful of optional colour themes are available if you want them — entirely optional, never required, and they never change the melody or your score. A free backup & restore is always available too — this is your data, not a premium feature.
