🧪 Hueful — Support

How do I play?

Tap a tube to pick it up, then tap another tube to pour. A pour only works if the destination is empty or its top color matches what you're pouring. Sort every color into its own tube (with the 2 spare empty tubes to help) to clear the level.

How do stars work?

Solving a level always earns at least 1 star. Solve it in fewer pours for 2 or 3 stars — every level has a target pour count based on how it was generated. Your best result for each level is saved.

My progress disappeared

Your level progress and stars are stored locally on your device only, nothing is backed up to a server. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage from your phone's settings, permanently removes it. Reinstalling starts fresh by design — there's no account to sign back into and recover it from. Use Backup & Restore (the disk icon) beforehand if you want to keep it.

Is every level actually solvable?

Yes, always. Every level is generated by starting from an already-sorted board and "unsorting" it with random legal pours — so there's always a way back to sorted, no matter how scrambled it looks.

What does the free plan include?

Every level is free to play, forever — no cap, no ads, no timers. "Hueful Pro" is a one-time unlock for 3 extra cosmetic color packs (Pastel, Neon, Midnight). Purely visual, never required to play or solve any level.

Can I use this on more than one device?

Not yet — each installation keeps its own separate local progress, with no sync between devices. This is a deliberate, current limitation rather than a bug.
