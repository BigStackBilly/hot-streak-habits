Thirsty Leaves helps you keep houseplants alive without turning it into another app to babysit.

Add a plant, pick an icon, and set how often it likes water — daily for a thirsty fern, monthly for a cactus, anything in between. Each plant shows its real condition at a glance: thriving, due today, wilting, or needing help — and a moisture bar that drains down to the day it's due.

WHY IT'S DIFFERENT

- No login, no account, no cloud — everything stays on your device
- No ads, no tracking, nothing sold
- A genuine overwatering guard — try to water a plant days early and the app checks in first, because overwatering (not forgetting) is the #1 real way houseplants die
- Health states you can see, not just a streak number — a plant visibly wilts the longer it goes overdue, and recovers the moment you water it
- No silent resets — if a plant's gone a while without water, you're asked to acknowledge it once before logging that watering, then it's back to normal
- Care streaks for on-time watering, tracked honestly, with your all-time best saved
- Free local backup and restore — your plants are never trapped on one device
- Gentle reminders when something's due — never more than once a day, never naggy
- Dark mode

BUILT TO STAY OUT OF YOUR WAY

This isn't a plant-identification app with AI scans and a subscription. It's fast enough to log a watering one-handed in under five seconds, and it stays quiet the rest of the time.

Free to start with up to 3 plants — plenty for a windowsill. Thirsty Leaves Pro removes that cap for anyone with a bigger collection, as a one-time unlock, not a subscription.
