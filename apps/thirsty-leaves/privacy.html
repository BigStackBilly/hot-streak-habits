🪴 Thirsty Leaves — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

Thirsty Leaves is a plant watering tracker built to work without collecting your personal data. This page explains exactly what does and doesn't happen with your information.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no account, no login, and no server that Thirsty Leaves sends your data to. The plants you add — their names, icons, watering plans, light notes, and watering history — are stored only in your device's local storage. We never see this information, and it never leaves your device.

What we don't do

  • No account creation or login of any kind
  • No advertising or ad tracking
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No selling or sharing of data, because none is collected

Notifications

Reminders are generated and shown entirely on your own device based on your plant data already stored there. No notification content is sent to or through any server we operate.

In-app purchases

The optional "Pro" unlock (unlimited plants), when available for purchase, is processed entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play. We do not receive, see, or store your payment details — that's handled directly between you and the platform you downloaded the app from.

Third-party services

The app loads its fonts and core libraries from third-party content delivery networks (Google Fonts, unpkg). These providers may log standard technical information such as IP address and browser type as part of serving those files, under their own privacy policies. We don't control or receive this information ourselves.

Children's privacy

Thirsty Leaves is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, regardless of age — since it doesn't collect information at all.

If you delete the app

Uninstalling the app or clearing your browser's site data removes all locally stored plant data permanently. We have no copy to restore, since we never had one.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future feature like cloud sync introduces an account or server-side storage — this page will be updated first, with a new "last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hotstreakhabits@gmail.com.

This page is intentionally short because the app itself is intentionally simple: everything stays on your device, always.
