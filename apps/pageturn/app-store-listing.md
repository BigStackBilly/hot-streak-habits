## Description

```
Pageturn keeps track of what you're reading without turning it into another chore.

Add a book — title, author, total pages — and log progress as you go, either by typing the exact page you're on or tapping a quick "+10 pages" chip. Every book shows a real progress bar and percentage, so you always know how far you are. Finish a book and it moves to your finished shelf with the date, and you can optionally leave a star rating and a one-line note.

WHY IT'S DIFFERENT

- No login, no account, no cloud — everything stays on your device
- No ads, no tracking, nothing sold
- No external lookups — add any book by hand in seconds, nothing sent anywhere
- No punishing daily streak — reading doesn't happen every day for most people, so Pageturn tracks a simple lifetime count of days you've logged a session instead of a number that resets and guilt-trips you for a normal gap
- A genuine finished shelf — every book you complete, with the date and your own rating/note, not just a checkbox
- Real stats: pages read today, pages this week, books finished this year
- Free local backup and restore — your library is never trapped on one device
- Unlimited books on the free tier — the core tracking is never gated
- Dark mode

BUILT TO STAY OUT OF YOUR WAY

This isn't a social reading app with feeds and follower counts. It's fast enough to log a reading session one-handed in a few seconds, and it stays quiet the rest of the time.

Pageturn Pro is an optional one-time unlock for a few extra accent color themes and an extended pages-per-month stats chart — never required, and never in the way of actually tracking your reading.

Add your first book today.
```
