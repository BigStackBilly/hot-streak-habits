📖 Pageturn — Support

My books/progress disappeared

All data is stored locally on your device only, nothing is backed up to a server. This means uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage from your phone's settings, permanently removes your book data. Reinstalling a fresh copy of the app starts empty by design — there's no account to sign back into and recover it from. Use Backup & Restore (the 💾 icon) beforehand to save your own copy.

How do I log pages I've read?

Tap "Log progress" on a book's card, then either type the exact page you're now on, or use the "+5 / +10 / +25 / +50 pages" quick-add chips to bump it up, and save. The progress bar and percentage update immediately.

How does a book get marked finished?

Once your logged page reaches the book's total page count, a "Mark as finished" button appears, which opens a small congratulations screen where you can optionally add a star rating and a one-line note. It only ever shows up for a real completion — never automatically for partial progress.

Why isn't there a daily streak?

Most people don't read every single day, and a streak that breaks over a normal gap just teaches people to stop opening the app. Instead, Pageturn tracks a simple lifetime count of "days with a reading session logged" — it only ever goes up, there's nothing to lose by taking a few days off.

Can I use this on more than one device?

Not directly — each installation keeps its own separate local data, with no automatic sync between devices. Use Backup & Restore to manually move your library from one device to another.

How do I delete a book?

For a currently-reading book, tap its card to open the edit screen, then "Delete book" at the bottom. For a finished book, tap the trash icon on its row in the finished shelf.

What does the free plan include?

Everything that actually tracks your reading is free and unlimited — add as many books as you like, log progress, finish books, and use backup/restore, with no caps. "Pageturn Pro" is a one-time unlock for a few extra accent color themes and an extended pages-per-month stats chart; see the Pro screen (crown icon) for details.
