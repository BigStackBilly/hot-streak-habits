Rankly gives you three rounds a day. Each one hands you five things and a scale — biggest to smallest, oldest to newest, slowest to fastest — and you put them in order.

WHY IT'S DIFFERENT

- No login, no account, no cloud — everything stays on your device
- No ads, no tracking, nothing sold
- Partial credit for every item in the right place, so a near miss still counts
- The correct order is revealed after every round, so you actually learn something
- The same three rounds for everyone each day
- Unlimited free practice sets whenever you want
- Dark mode and light mode, your choice

A handful of optional colour themes are available if you want them — entirely optional, never required, and they never change the rounds or your score. A free backup & restore is always available too — this is your data, not a premium feature.

Three rounds. Fifteen decisions.
