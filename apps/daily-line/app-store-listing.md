## Description

```
Daily Line makes journaling take ten seconds, not ten minutes.

Every day you get one short prompt — a nudge, not a homework assignment — and one line to fill in. Pick a mood, write your sentence, tap save. Come back tomorrow and do it again.

WHY IT'S DIFFERENT

- No login, no account, no cloud — everything stays on your device
- No ads, no tracking, nothing sold
- One line, one prompt, one tap — no blank-page pressure
- Prompts are optional inspiration, never mandatory — skip to another anytime, or ignore them and write whatever's on your mind
- A real streak for consecutive days written — it can break, and that's okay, no guilt trips or dialogs to click through, just a quiet nudge to start again
- A month calendar that fills in like a heatmap as you write, so you can see your journaling pattern at a glance
- Tap any past day to read what you wrote
- Dark mode, because a lot of journaling happens right before bed

BUILT TO STAY OUT OF YOUR WAY

This isn't a full journaling app with tags, folders, and a rich text editor. It's fast enough to use one-handed in under fifteen seconds, and it stays quiet the rest of the time.

Optional prompt packs (Gratitude, Career & Growth, Relationships) are available if you want more focused inspiration — entirely optional, never required. An optional Pro export lets you download everything you've written as a plain text file whenever you want.

Start today's line.
```
