― Daily Line — Support

My entries/streak disappeared

All data is stored locally on your device only, nothing is backed up to a server. This means uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage from your phone's settings, permanently removes your entries. Reinstalling a fresh copy of the app starts empty by design — there's no account to sign back into and recover it from.

Why does my streak show 0?

A streak counts consecutive days with an entry. If a day goes by with nothing written, the streak resets — that's by design, not a bug. Writing a line today always starts a fresh streak.

Can I change today's prompt?

Yes — tap the shuffle icon (🔀) next to the prompt to see another one. Prompts are just inspiration; you never have to answer them literally, and you can write anything you like in the line below.

Can I edit a past entry?

Right now you can only write or update today's line — tapping a filled day on the calendar lets you read it back, but not edit it yet.

Can I use this on more than one device?

Not yet — each installation keeps its own separate local data, with no sync between devices. This is a deliberate, current limitation rather than a bug.

How do I export my entries?

Unlock Daily Line Pro from the 🎁 shop button in the header, then tap "Export" to download everything you've written as a plain text file.

Something's broken, or I have a question not covered here

Email hotstreakhabits@gmail.com and describe what happened, including your phone model and iOS version if it's a display or crash issue.
