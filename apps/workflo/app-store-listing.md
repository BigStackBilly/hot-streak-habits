## Description

```
Workflo is a deep-work timer built around the classic work/break interval technique: focus for a stretch, take a short break, repeat — with a longer break every few cycles to actually recover.

Start with the classic 25 minutes of focus / 5 minute break rhythm, or adjust every interval (focus length, short break, long break, and how many cycles before a long break) to fit how you work. A big countdown ring shows exactly where you are in the current phase, with a clear "Focus" / "Short break" / "Long break" label so you never have to guess. When a phase ends, Workflo transitions automatically with a sound and a vibration — and if you're mid-flow and don't need the break, one tap skips it and gets you back to work.

WHY IT'S DIFFERENT

- The full timer is completely free, always — no ads, no paywall on the actual focus/break mechanic
- No login, no account, no cloud — everything stays on your device
- A session log tracks your completed focus time by day, so you can see "focused for 2h 15m today" — not a punishing streak that guilts you on days you don't sit down to work
- An optional, lightweight "what are you working on" label per session, entirely optional and fast to skip
- Auto-transitions between focus and break with a sound + vibration, but you're always in control — pause, resume, skip a break, or reset
- Light and dark themes, with a phase-adaptive accent color so you can tell focus and break apart at a glance

BUILT TO STAY OUT OF YOUR WAY

No accounts, no forced setup wizard before your first session, no ads interrupting a focus block. Open it, hit start, work.

A couple of optional ring/accent color themes and your full session history plus a weekly focus chart are available via Workflo Pro — entirely optional, never required to use the timer itself.

Start your next focus block in one tap.
```
