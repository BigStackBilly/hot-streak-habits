WorkFlowStack is a classic Pomodoro-style focus timer — 25 minutes on, 5 off, a longer break every fourth round — paired with an honest log of the hours you actually spent focused.

WHY IT'S DIFFERENT

- No login, no account, no cloud — everything stays on your device
- No ads, no tracking, nothing sold
- A plain, honest hours-logged record — no gamified trees, no streaks to protect
- Configurable work/break lengths if the classic 25/5 isn't your pace
- Dark mode and light mode, your choice

A handful of optional cosmetic timer-ring themes and a longer history view are available if you want them — entirely optional, never required. A free backup & restore is always available too — this is your data, not a premium feature.
