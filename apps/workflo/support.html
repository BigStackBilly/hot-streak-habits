⏱️ WorkFlowStack — Support

How do the intervals work?

WorkFlowStack defaults to the classic Pomodoro rhythm: 25 minutes of focus, then a 5-minute short break, repeating — with a longer 15-minute break every 4th cycle. You can change any of these lengths (focus, short break, long break, and how many cycles before a long break) from the gear icon.

My saved focus history disappeared

All data is stored locally on your device only, nothing is backed up to a server. This means uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage from your phone's settings, permanently removes your settings and session history. Reinstalling a fresh copy of the app starts empty by design — there's no account to sign back into and recover it from. Use the Backup & restore option (the 💾 icon in the header) beforehand if you want your own copy.

Why didn't a completed break/focus interval get logged?

Only completed focus intervals are logged toward your totals — breaks are never logged, since they're not work. A focus interval only counts once its countdown reaches zero naturally; ending a session early with Reset doesn't log a partial cycle.

What does WorkFlowStack Pro unlock?

A few extra ring/accent color themes, plus your full session history and a weekly focus chart beyond the free 7-day window. The core timer — focus/break intervals, the countdown ring, and today's totals — is always free and never limited.

Can I use this on more than one device?

Not yet — each installation keeps its own separate local data, with no sync between devices. You can move data between devices manually using Backup & restore.
