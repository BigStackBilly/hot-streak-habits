Wordday is a five-letter word puzzle — one word a day, the same for everyone, six guesses to find it.

Green means right letter, right spot. Yellow means it's in the word, wrong spot. Six tries to work it out, then come back tomorrow for a new one.

WHY IT'S DIFFERENT

- No login, no account, no cloud — everything stays on your device
- No ads, no tracking, nothing sold
- Unlimited free Endless mode for whenever you want more than one puzzle a day
- A real streak for consecutive days solved
- Full statistics: games played, win rate, and your guess distribution
- Copy your result as a shareable grid, no spoilers
- Dark mode and light mode, your choice

BUILT TO STAY OUT OF YOUR WAY

One puzzle, one board, no ads between rounds, no forced tutorial. A handful of optional tile-color themes are available if you want to change how the board looks — entirely optional, never required. A free backup & restore is always available too — this is your data, not a premium feature.
