## Description

```
Bubblio is a classic bubble-shooter puzzle: aim, shoot, and pop groups of 3 or more matching bubbles before you run out of shots.

HOW TO PLAY

Drag to aim, release to fire the bubble waiting at the bottom. Land it next to matching colors to pop a group — and watch any bubbles left floating with nothing holding them up drop away too. Clear every bubble on the board before your shots run out to finish the level.

WHY IT'S DIFFERENT

- 30 real levels, each with its own board and shot budget — not just one endless mode
- An honest aim guide — the line shows exactly where your shot lands, wall bounces included, not just the direction you're pulling
- Earn 1-3 stars per level based on how efficiently you clear it, with your best result always saved
- No login, no account, no cloud — everything stays on your device
- No ads, no tracking, nothing sold
- No lives to wait out, no energy timer, no forced ads between levels
- Optional cosmetic bubble color packs (Sherbet, Neon, Midnight) if you want to customize the look — entirely optional, never required
- Free local backup and restore, so your level progress is never trapped on one device
- Dark mode

BUILT TO STAY OUT OF YOUR WAY

Tap in, clear a level, tap out — no forced tutorials, no popups begging for a review.

Pop your first bubble today.
```
