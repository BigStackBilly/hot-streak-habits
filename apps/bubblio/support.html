🫧 Bubblio — Support

How do I play?

Drag anywhere on the board to aim, then release to shoot the bubble shown at the bottom. The dashed guide line shows exactly where that shot will land, wall bounces included — not just the direction you're pulling. Land it next to 2+ bubbles of the same color to pop 3 or more at once. Popping a group can also drop any bubbles left floating with nothing connecting them to the top. Clear the whole board before you run out of shots to complete the level.

How do stars work?

Clearing a level always earns at least 1 star. Finish with plenty of shots to spare for 2 or 3 stars — the fewer shots you use, the more stars you earn. Your best result for each level is saved.

My progress disappeared

Your level progress and stars are stored locally on your device only, nothing is backed up to a server. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage from your phone's settings, permanently removes it. Reinstalling starts fresh by design — there's no account to sign back into and recover it from. Use Backup & Restore (the disk icon) beforehand if you want to keep it.

What does the free plan include?

Every level is free to play, forever — no cap, no ads, no timers. "Bubblio Pro" is a one-time unlock for 3 extra cosmetic bubble color packs (Sherbet, Neon, Midnight). Purely visual, never required to play or clear any level.

Can I use this on more than one device?

Not yet — each installation keeps its own separate local progress, with no sync between devices. This is a deliberate, current limitation rather than a bug.
